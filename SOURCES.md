## Social attitudes and contemporary Japan

- Japan Cabinet Office. [August 2025 National Life Survey summary (PDF)](https://survey.gov-online.go.jp/202509/r07/r07-life/gairyaku.pdf). Question 18 and its response table support the ideal-job figures in chapter 1.
- Institute of Statistical Mathematics. [Surveys on the Japanese National Character](https://www.ism.ac.jp/ism_info_e/kokuminsei_e.html). Background on repeated studies of attitudes and values.
- Statistics Bureau of Japan. [Statistical Handbook of Japan](https://www.stat.go.jp/english/data/handbook/). Demographic, labor and social context.

## Daily life, language and customs

- Japan National Tourism Organization. [Manners and etiquette](https://www.japan.travel/en/guide/understanding-and-mastering-japanese-manners-and-etiquette/), [bathing](https://www.japan.travel/en/guide/bathing-manners-and-tips/), [shrines and temples](https://www.japan.travel/en/guide/shrine-and-temple-traditions/) and [holidays](https://www.japan.travel/en/plan/business-hours-and-holidays/). Visitor-facing descriptions of common customs.
- Tokyo Metro. [Subway manners](https://www.tokyometro.jp/en/tips/mannervideo/index.html) and [things to consider while riding](https://www.tokyometro.jp/en/tips/considerations/index.html). Operator-specific guidance.
- Immigration Services Agency. [Guidebook on Living and Working](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/support/portal/guidebook_all.html). Multilingual resident guidance, including daily life and local procedures.
- Japan Foundation. [Irodori Japanese for Life in Japan](https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/en/). Practical Japanese lessons and audio.
- Tokyo Metropolitan Government. [Multilingual consultation](https://www.seikatubunka.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/about/0000002541) and [disaster preparedness](https://tokyo-resilience.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/en/prevention/). Tokyo-specific support.
- Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries. [Our Regional Cuisines](https://www.maff.go.jp/e/policies/market/k_ryouri/index.html). Regional food and its history.
- Upopoy. [National Ainu Museum](https://ainu-upopoy.go.jp/en/museum/). Ainu history and culture.
- Okinawa’s official travel guide. [Local culture and Ryukyu heritage](https://visitokinawajapan.com/travel-inspiration/local-culture-in-world-heritage-okinawa/). Historical and regional context.

## Work and employment

- Japan External Trade Organization. [Improving workplace communication](https://www.jetro.go.jp/hrportal/forcompanies/active/communication.html) and [business communication playbook (PDF)](https://www.jetro.go.jp/ext_images/en/jgc/reports/2026/05bfbb92550e0ea2/Playbook.pdf). The first addresses multilingual teams in Japan. The playbook discusses selected business interactions in a Japan–U.S. context.
- Toyota Motor Corporation. [Toyota Production System background](https://www.toyota-global.com/company/history_of_toyota/75years/data/automotive_business/production/system/change.html). An example of one organization’s approach.
- Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare. [Labor standards and Working Conditions Handbook](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/koyou_roudou/roudoukijun/foreign/index.html), [worker dispatch](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/koyou_roudou/roudoushahakennjigyou.html), [harassment guidance (PDF)](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/11900000/001225731.pdf) and [foreign-language consultation](https://www.check-roudou.mhlw.go.jp/soudan/foreigner_eng.html). Official resources for employment conditions and support.

## History, education and leisure

- National Diet Library. [Modern Japan in Archives](https://www.ndl.go.jp/modern/e/). Historical documents and context through the postwar period.
- Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology. [School enrollment guide (PDF)](https://www.mext.go.jp/a_menu/nihongo_kyoiku/kyoiku/handbook/pdf/en_zensho.pdf). School system and practical information for families.
- Statistics Bureau of Japan. [2021 leisure activity results (PDF)](https://www.stat.go.jp/english/data/shakai/2021/pdf/activities2021.pdf). Table 4-3 gives participation by activity.
