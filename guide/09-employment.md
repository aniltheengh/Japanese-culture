# Employment and support

[Home](../README.md) · [Workplace etiquette](07-workplace.md) · [Phrasebook](10-phrasebook.md)

Use your written conditions and current Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) materials for employment rules. Confirm the details for your work arrangement when you have a specific case.

| Topic | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Written conditions | Employer, job and workplace, working days and hours, pay, overtime, leave, and contact for concerns |
| Timekeeping | How to record all time worked, how the company authorizes overtime, and how to correct a missing entry |
| Leave | Which system to use, who receives the request and what handover is needed |
| Illness or delays | Who must be notified and by what channel. Share practical details such as expected availability. |
| Harassment | Which internal contact accepts complaints and what external consultation is available |
| Safety | Site induction, required training or protective equipment, hazard reporting and emergency contacts |
| Resignation or contract changes | Contract terms, written notices, and the appropriate support service if there is a dispute |

When someone asks you to stay late, clarify the deliverable, urgency, authorization and time record. If several urgent tasks conflict, ask the responsible person to confirm priority. For overtime limits, pay and leave eligibility, check the MHLW handbook or ask the consultation service using your dates, schedule and employment terms. [MHLW Working Conditions Handbook and labor standards resources](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/koyou_roudou/roudoukijun/foreign/index.html)

## Dispatch employment

In worker dispatch, **派遣元 (はけんもと, hakenmoto)** is the employing agency and **派遣先 (はけんさき, hakensaki)** is the host that directs day-to-day work. Confirm who approves time and leave, who receives absence notices, and where to send questions about duties or changes in the assignment. In a site emergency, follow the host’s immediate procedure and the relevant reporting instructions. For contract, payroll or assignment issues, start with the agency contact and involve the host contact when its action is needed. Your documents may name designated contacts on both sides. [MHLW worker-dispatch overview](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/koyou_roudou/roudoushahakennjigyou.html) · [MHLW sample written conditions](https://jsite.mhlw.go.jp/aichi-foreigner/library/aichi-foreigner/gaikokujin/jp/pdf/jigyounusi/syugyoujyoukennmeijisyo-en.pdf)

## When something goes wrong

For repeated harassment, unpaid work or a dispute over working conditions, note dates, hours, what happened, who was involved and any documents you are allowed to retain. Ask the relevant HR, agency or labor consultation contact about your options. MHLW publishes separate resources for workplace harassment and labor standards and lists foreign-language services, including Nepali. Check the current hours and whether the number is free or paid before calling. [MHLW harassment examples and consultation guidance](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/11900000/001225731.pdf) · [Nepali consultation information](https://www.check-roudou.mhlw.go.jp/soudan/foreigner_npl.html) · [Labour Standards Advice Hotline](https://www.check-roudou.mhlw.go.jp/lp/hotline/hotline_eng.html)
