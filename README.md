## Quick reference

- **On a train:** Queue at the markings, let passengers exit first, and keep calls for later.
- **Visiting a home:** Confirm the time, remove shoes at the entrance, and ask where to place them if unsure.
- **Given a task at work:** Confirm the output, due date, drawing or document revision, and reviewer.
- **A deadline is at risk:** Tell the affected person early, explain the impact, and offer an updated plan.
- **A colleague says “we’ll consider it”:** Record it as pending and confirm the next decision date.
- **Unsure which custom applies:** Look for signs, follow the host’s lead, or ask. Local rules, company procedures and individual preferences all matter.

The scenarios are illustrative. Official resident, labor and venue guidance is linked where applicable.
